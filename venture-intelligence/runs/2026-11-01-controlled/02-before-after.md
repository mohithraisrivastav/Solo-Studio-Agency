## What this shows

- The judgment is better. The hunters now find the competitors the audits found, reach the same verdicts the independent audits reached, and show their reasoning when a verdict depends on a guess.
- The system still has not found an exceptional opportunity. Eighteen ideas ended in one weak INVESTIGATE. This is the rejection pattern you warned about, now with better evidence behind it.
- The cause is visible in the failure line of nearly every idea: "Large enough, within means". Both hunters worked inside small niches (honey, agarwood, casks, footwear labs). The pools were too small for one person to earn well, so careful analysis kept rejecting them. The weakness has moved from judging ideas to choosing where to look.
