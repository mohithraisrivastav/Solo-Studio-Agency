## Selected for independent verification (5)

1. **A1 Gold loss in karigar workshops.** Large money per buyer, a beatable trust barrier with a payer (the wholesaler) who gains directly, and no Indian loss-audit service found. Highest differentiation room.
2. **C1 Shared supplier audits for small drug makers.** A compulsory task that recent enforcement makes urgent, low capital, and a shared-cost model proven abroad. **Contested:** hunter B cut pharma Schedule M services as crowded consulting (`01-hunt-B.md` cuts R5 to R7). Verification must test whether this is consulting under another name and whether inspectors accept shared reports.
3. **A2 Re-dyeing in job-work dye houses.** Rework is paid for every day and an outcome-priced service is unusual in the trade. Who bears the re-dye cost is unconfirmed and decides the payer.
4. **B3 Hospital oxygen plant upkeep.** Real tenders show payment for upkeep, and the installed base is out of warranty. High risk that payers do not budget; verification must test private hospitals as payers.
5. **C3 Lift accountability for housing societies.** Strong, recent pain and legal pressure. No evidence that societies pay for monitoring; verification must test willingness to pay and maker resistance.

These span five unrelated industries.

## Dropped, with reasons

- **A3 Foundry energy.** Performance and energy-service contracts have a documented record of disputes and inactivity, and owners are used to subsidised audits. Weakest willingness-to-pay evidence of the operations theses.
- **B1 C&D waste compliance.** The three-year carry-forward lets builders defer, enforcement data for 2026 was not found, and recycled material has a history of not selling. Revisit when enforcement data appears.
- **B2 CBAM data.** Crowded with vendors and testing majors; the hunter itself rates the differentiation easy to copy; a government subsidy may route buyers to accredited providers.
- **C2 Estate settlement.** Regulation is fixing the bank part, several funded or recent rivals exist, and one-time purchase with unknown acquisition cost.
