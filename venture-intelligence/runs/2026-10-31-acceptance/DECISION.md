## The one action worth money now

Gold field test, under ₹50,000, 6-8 weeks: assay 10 pooled workshop lots in one cluster, record what owners are paid today and how, and ask three licensed refiners for written terms on assay-based settlement of 25-50 g lots. Kill if pooled lots usually hold under about 25 g, if owners already realise more than 85%, or if refiners refuse assay settlement (`10-critique-1-gold.md`). The user approves and runs any contact with workshops or refiners.

Pharma shared supplier audits (small market, regulator acting, `03-market-2`); hospital oxygen plant upkeep (plants idle because unneeded, `03-market-4`); foundry energy, C&D waste compliance, CBAM data, estate settlement (`02-triage.md`).

## What the revised system found (round 2, hunter D)

`01-hunt-D.md`, run on the revised hunter instructions with a product lens, kept three physical-product theses:
1. **Battery-buffered induction stations for eateries.** After the 2026 commercial LPG shock, eateries moving to induction can cross an electricity tariff step; a battery that caps grid draw is worth about ₹3.5 lakh a year to a one-cylinder-a-day eatery in Pune (inference from a non-official tariff guide). No Indian product found; US home products prove the concept. Deciding unknown: whether the power company counts load behind a battery as connected load. Problem spend L1; solution L0.
2. **Humidity-controlled ventilation retrofit for onion storage sheds.** About 30% losses reported in 2026; trial results mixed; no retrofit kit found. Needs one season of side-by-side bays.
3. **Village-scale makhana popping machine.** Weakest: machines exist with 50-60% subsidy.

Status: not yet verified by an independent Market Investigator or screened commercially. That is the next step, before any invention.
