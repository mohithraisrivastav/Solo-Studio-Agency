# Independent audit of the five shortlisted ideas

Date: 2026-11-01. Five independent critics researched each idea without contacting anyone. Sources are search summaries only (page fetching and most Indian government hosts were blocked), so "verified" below means seen in a search result, not read at source. Details are in `10-audit-1` to `10-audit-5`.

| Idea | Hunter's outcome | Audit outcome | What was found | Verified | Still a hypothesis or missing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cask passport | INVESTIGATE, "no Indian product" | INVESTIGATE, narrowed, low priority | SmartCask by Checkpoint (CCL Label) already sells a digital passport with an RFID chip for identity and custody. Level and weight sensing has wide prior art (bung sensors, patents, Inspectahire gauge, Kigtek). Sensiolink and CaskEye were not found. Only two Indian cask programmes verified (Diageo, Paul John). Entry price near Rs 30 lakh. | Prior art exists. Programmes are few. | Excise rule on fitting devices; sensor accuracy in a bung; whether owners want level data. Tag capex for 450 casks about Rs 33 lakh against Rs 5 lakh capital (estimate). |
| Honey pre-screen | INVESTIGATE | REJECT as proposed | NMR is an export rule, not a domestic one. RAFT is FSSAI's approval scheme for on-the-spot kits, not a honey programme. Designed "all-pass" syrups pass C3/C4; portable NIR quantifies adulterant only above about 10 to 20 percent. A pre-screen has no legal weight. | The legal frame, the technical limit. | NMR fee (Rs 15,000 to 25,000 unverified), failure rate, calibration library cost. The hunter's saving per lot was wrong: Rs 450, not Rs 775. |
| Groundwater meter rental | INVESTIGATE | INVESTIGATE, leaning reject | Meter plus telemetry is mandatory for NOC holders. No ownership rule, approved-make list, or rental precedent found. Buyer's own cost of owning is about Rs 1,048 a month; the vendor break-even is about Rs 1,457, so there is no saving to share. 100 meters need about Rs 27 lakh. | The mandate. | Ownership rules, calibration prices, demand (L0). The 12,650 figure counts permits, not buyers. |
| Broiler live-haul kit | INVESTIGATE | INVESTIGATE, low confidence | The same study says the seasonal difference in shrink was not significant, which the hunter omitted. Most early loss is fasting, which a kit cannot fix. At 5 percent recovery the gain is Rs 475 to 875 a trip. A kit costs about Rs 24,500 and sells near Rs 41,000 (estimate); 50 kits need about Rs 20 lakh. | The Chennai figures. | Who bears the shrink in open trade; the heat-avoidable share. |
| Aroma authenticity lab | INVESTIGATE | INVESTIGATE, leaning weak | The quota is real (DGFT 52/2024-25). No grading standard exists for agarwood. GC-MS testing is already sold at about Rs 500 to 4,500 a sample. At the one reported export (112 kg) a lab would see about 11 to 45 samples a year against 583 needed. | The quota; the price of testing. | Gulf acceptance of Indian reports; price premium for graded lots. |

None of the five is ready to advance. One is rejected as proposed. The other four stay open only because the deciding unknowns could not be read at source, not because there is evidence for them.
