# Hunt A: money wasted (draft, deciding questions written before searching)

Deciding questions per industry:
- Marine fisheries post-harvest: Is on-boat spoilage large enough to pay for a fix, or is ice a small cost? Do subsidies already cover insulated boxes? Who keeps the saving (auction agent or boat owner)?
- Poultry egg logistics: How much breakage and who bears it (farmer, trader, retailer)? Is there a price-allowance contract that makes the loser unable to benefit? Which crate/tray makers already sell a fix?
- Port and container logistics: Who pays detention, is it avoidable by the buyer, and does a digital tool already exist?
- Industrial steam and compressed air in SMEs: Is the leak loss measured, and do equipment makers already sell survey and monitoring?
