Date: 2026-11-01. Files relied on: `01-hunt-A/B/C.md`, `02-hunt-A/B/C-r2.md`, and the screens `../2026-11-01-screen-induction-onion/03-market-1-induction.md`, `03-market-2-onion.md`.

## What happened

- Round 1 (rules that cut on any unsupported buyer question): 3 hunters, 0 ideas kept, about 36 candidates cut. Several cuts rested on missing evidence or blocked sources, not on contradiction.
- Round 2 (three outcomes, creative mandate): 24 new ideas and 38 re-sorted candidates. Result: 0 ADVANCE, many INVESTIGATE, 13 REJECT (4 by hunter B, 8 by hunter C, 1 by hunter A; each with stated reason).
- No buyer was contacted. Every source is a search summary, because page fetching is blocked. Demand evidence is L0 to L1 for every idea.

Most ideas are small (a few lakh to a few tens of lakh a year), and INVESTIGATE can become a parking place. Sourced share fell to 38 to 48 percent because most numbers are now hypotheses, which is honest but means nothing has been proven.

## What this shows about the system

- Fixed in this round: the system no longer rejects on missing evidence, and it generates ideas where no market exists. The kill probe, prior-art sweep, and payback script stay.
- Still weak: no primary rule text could be read (blocked hosts), so every legal claim is `UNVERIFIED KILLER`. Idea sizes are small. Ranking was done by me, not by an independent specialist.
- Next step needs a decision from the user: approve any of tests 1 to 5. Until then, nothing here is a recommendation to invest.
