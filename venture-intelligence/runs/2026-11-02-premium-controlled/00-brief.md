# Brief: second controlled discovery run

Date: 2026-11-01 (run folder dated for the next cycle). User approval: one new controlled run.

Objective: discover exceptional business opportunities with meaningful originality, high commercial potential, and a credible path for an independent founder starting with Rs 0 capital. Premium track: original products that could sell for Rs 5 to 10 lakh per piece (distinctive design, material innovation, exclusive ownership, a compelling reason to buy).

Three independent approaches: (A) market intelligence, (B) multidisciplinary invention then expert review, (C) commercial opportunity discovery. Invention is not limited to problems with documented spending. The seven opportunity standards apply after concept generation. Size-first check by business model. Capital Rs 0: explore buyer-funded development, licensing, partnerships. Global novelty search for shortlisted ideas, including patents, products, failed ventures, non-English markets. Consequential claims need source documents opened; search summaries cannot support ADVANCE. Independent critics check leaders without inheriting hunters' conclusions. No forced winner.

No contact with buyers, no prototypes, no spending, no launches.

Excluded industries (used in earlier runs; revisit only with new evidence of a significant opportunity): gold and jewellery, textile dyeing, residential lifts, hospital oxygen, pharma audits, foundries, construction waste, carbon border reporting, estate settlement, commercial cooking and induction, onion storage, makhana, groundwater and water metering, refrigerants and cooling, footwear and leather testing, tyres and rubber, honey and food authenticity, animal feed byproducts, poultry transport, marine fisheries, truck tyres, dairy packaging, port logistics, industrial steam, spices, whisky and cask, agarwood, attar and sandalwood, para-sport equipment, archives and heritage preservation.

Access note (2026-11-01): web page fetching fails for every host tested (en.wikipedia.org, patents.google.com, cgwb.gov.in). Search works. Until the user allows hosts, sources are seen through search summaries, so no idea can reach ADVANCE in this run.
