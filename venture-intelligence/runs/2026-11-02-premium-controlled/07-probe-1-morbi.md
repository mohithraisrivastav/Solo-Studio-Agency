## Verdict

**INVESTIGATE, weak.** It would be REJECT on novelty alone. The idea is not original. A machine that taps ceramic parts and analyses the sound for fissures was patented in 2001, and the same principle is published for tiles. Only three things keep it alive: (1) no commercial acoustic tile or sanitaryware crack station was found, though absence after a desk search is not proof [I]; (2) the economics work only if hidden-crack losses are about 1% of tiles or more, and no source on that exists; (3) the payer's budget line is unknown.

**Deciding fact (tested first):** is there a measurable, paid-for hidden-crack loss in Morbi that a Rs 6 lakh station would recover [H]? Not found after 6 searches (queries: "Morbi ceramic tile rejection percentage breakage returns claims", "Morbi tile manufacturers hidden cracks sorting manual tapping", "Morbi vitrified tiles hairline crack after laying complaint", Hindi sorter wage query, "Morbi sanitaryware factories", "tile hidden crack startup closed"). Missing information is not proof of no demand, and it is not evidence of demand.

**Even-if-true test.** I assumed the open points resolve well: crack escape 1% of tiles, false rejects 0.3%, Rs 500 cost per escaped crack [A]. Buyer saving is then about Rs 76.6 lakh a year per station, payback under 1 month [I, script `07-probe-1-calc.py`, a favourable case, not a result]. Headings 6 and 5 still fail or stay weak: nothing defends the product (Heading 6), and the buying channel is held by Sacmi (Heading 5). Capital at 100 customers is about Rs 1.5 crore of parts against Rs 0 (Heading 7). It does not clearly pass 5 to 7 even if true, which is why the outcome is a weak INVESTIGATE and not ADVANCE. If the cheap test below shows low crack cost or no willing pilot payer, REJECT.

## 8. Reasons buyers would not switch

- Sacmi has a Morbi technical hub, a 175-person India team and an installed base claim of 950 to 1,000 plants; a small supplier has no service network [S8] [unverified at source].
- Calibration risk: resonance methods need a trained reference set and periodic recalibration [S16] [unverified at source]; the pipe patent cites firing degree, porosity and moisture shifting the sound [S21] [unverified at source].
- Tile acoustics depends on where the tile is struck, with large variance by position [S27] [unverified at source].
- Cost pressure: gas shock and up-to-40% cost rise make owners cut spend [S5] [unverified at source].
- No standard demands it: IS 13630 tests cover dimensions, breaking strength, crazing, not a hidden-crack acoustic test [S28] [unverified at source].
- Consumer claims often blame installation, so makers may not feel the full cost [S12] [unverified at source].

## 10. Cheapest credible demand test (not run)

**The most important unknown:** what a hidden cracked tile that escapes the factory costs the factory per year, and whether the owner would pay for a pilot. That sits before any prototype or fee quote.

**Test 1 (settles the deciding doubt, no prototype):** short structured conversations with 10 quality or dispatch heads in Morbi (tile and sanitaryware), found through public trade-association member lists and Sacmi Morbi or lab contacts (Asian Quality Services, Parul University testing). Ask for last-12-month figures: cracked-tile claims by value, share of dispatch, how cracks are checked at packing, who tapped, how many people, and the wage. Offer nothing and sell nothing.
- **Success:** at least 3 of 10 report hidden-crack claim cost above Rs 10 lakh a year per plant (roughly the 1% crack rate where the base case turns positive [I]), AND at least 2 say they would host an unpaid bench trial with their own known-cracked tiles [H].
- **Failure (REJECT):** median claim cost under Rs 3 lakh a year [A], or cracks are handled by the dealer without charge to the plant.
- **Cost:** Rs 0 cash; about 20 founder hours = Rs 15,000 at Rs 750 [I]. **Time:** 2 to 3 weeks [A].

**Test 2 (only if Test 1 succeeds, cost under Rs 3,000):** hammer and phone microphone on 100 tiles (50 with known cracks, from a lab or a factory reject pile) to see whether crack and no-crack separate in a spectrum [H]. This answers the engineering risk (positioning, tile size, glaze, moisture). Success: detection above 85% at false reject under 2% [A]. Cost about Rs 3,000 plus 15 hours (Rs 11,250) [I]. I do not run it.
