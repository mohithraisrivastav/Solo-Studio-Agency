## The preserved concept and its decisive experiment

The clock is the only candidate that is both original in form and shown feasible by an independent expert. It is not an exceptional opportunity: the buyer count is unknown, the builder is unfound, and the cash need is five times the advance. Decisive experiments, none run:
1. Free: ask 10 purohits and trust treasurers what tithi error they accept, and what they would pay. Ask two watchmakers or tower-clock makers for a written build quote. Needs your approval to make any contact.
2. Rs 15,000 to 40,000: bench model driven at one simulated day per minute to measure cam lash and differential play against the 1 hour budget.
Kill it if buyers will not pay Rs 8 lakh, if no builder quotes under about Rs 6 lakh a unit, or if backlash exceeds the budget.

Hypothesis, not tested: an unknown founder with no capital and no brand cannot sell Rs 8 lakh objects on trust. A licence or partnership with an established maker may be the only route (see heading 6 and 7).
