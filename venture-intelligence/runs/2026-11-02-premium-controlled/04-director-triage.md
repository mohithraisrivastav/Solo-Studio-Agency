## Shortlist for independent critics (no hunter conclusions passed on)

Held for a cheap kill probe: Morbi tap-tone grader and scrap-CRGO check (the two B2B instruments). Dropped without further spend: acoustic jali (weak function), speculum telescope (small), acoustic fingerprint (crowded), magnetic table (weakest).
